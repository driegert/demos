The Monty Hall problem

Behind three doors are one car and two goats. You pick a door, then the host — who knows exactly where the car is — opens a different door and shows you a goat. Two doors remain closed: yours, and one other. Should you stay with your pick, or switch?

What to notice

  • Stay wins exactly when your first pick was the car — probability 1/3.
  • Switch wins exactly when your first pick was a goat — probability 2/3.
  • The two win counts always add up to the number of rounds played, because the host never opens the car.
